🫁 Lungs

The lungs are the main organs of the respiratory system. They absorb oxygen from the air and remove carbon dioxide from the body during breathing.

⬅ Back to Anatomy

Lungs Information

📍 Location

Located inside the chest on both sides of the heart.

⚙ Function

Supplies oxygen to the blood and removes carbon dioxide from the body.

💡 Interesting Fact

An adult breathes about 20,000 times every day.

🩺 Common Diseases

Asthma
Pneumonia
Lung Cancer
