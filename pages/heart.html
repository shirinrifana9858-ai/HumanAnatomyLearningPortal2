❤️ Heart

The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood throughout the body. It supplies oxygen and nutrients to all body organs and helps keep you alive.

⬅ Back to Anatomy

Heart Information

📍 Location

Inside the chest, slightly left of the center.

⚙ Function

Pumps oxygen-rich blood throughout the body.

💡 Interesting Fact

An adult heart beats about 100,000 times every day.

🩺 Common Diseases

Heart Attack
Coronary Artery Disease
Heart Failure
